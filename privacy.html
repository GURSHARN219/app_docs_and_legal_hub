Arrow Escape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Arrow Escape is operated by Gursharn Singh. This policy explains what the app stores and what information may be handled by its third-party services.

1. Information and local storage

Arrow Escape does not require an account and does not send game data to a server. The app stores level results, unlocked progress, endless streaks, and preferences locally on the device using browser or app storage. The saved preferences include sound, haptics/vibration, relaxed mode, reduced motion, and grid-line settings.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The app has no account, payment processing, or server-side game-data service. The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads to show banner ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, for hints or other in-game benefits). The ads SDK may automatically collect and share your IP address, app interactions such as launches, taps and ad views, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. Ads are initialized only on Android; the web version does not serve ads through this integration. The app developer does not receive your locally saved game data through the ad SDK.

Before requesting ads, the Android app requests advertising consent information and displays a consent form when required and available. You can review available choices through Settings → Ad privacy options. Your choices and the ads that can be served may depend on your location and the options provided by Google.

3. Fonts and open-source libraries

The web app requests font resources from Google Fonts. A browser may share technical request information with Google while retrieving those fonts. The app also uses open-source libraries including React, Vite, Lucide React, Motion, and canvas-confetti. Those libraries do not create an Arrow Escape account or remote game-data service.

4. Retention and deletion

Level progress can be cleared with Reset Game Progress. That action does not necessarily clear separately stored preferences or game-resource counters. To remove all Arrow Escape data stored on the device, clear the app's storage through your browser or device settings; this may also clear preferences and progress. There is no server-side copy of game progress and no account deletion endpoint. Data handled by Google for ads is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices; use the ad privacy controls and Android advertising ID controls where available.

5. Cookies, tracking, and children

The web version uses browser local storage for game data and does not intentionally set tracking cookies. Google Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; Google may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when fonts load. The Android ads SDK may use identifiers and other data as described above. Arrow Escape does not ask for age or date-of-birth information. Parents or guardians should supervise children using the app; advertising availability and treatment depend on the applicable platform and consent settings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or bug reports, contact gursharnbusiness@gmail.com. Include “Arrow Escape” and the app version in the subject.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Please check this page for the current version.
